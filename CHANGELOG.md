## [Unreleased] - Phase 0 Foundation

### Added
- Comprehensive initial documentation foundation:
  - Detailed `README.md` with vision, architecture overview, tech stack, proposed structure, roadmap, and initiator context (Sven Normen Esslinger / Esslinger & Co., Hannover).
  - `CONTRIBUTING.md` with guidelines spanning code (Rust/Python), mesh, blockchain, AI agents, hardware prototyping, creative work, monitoring, and business/governance contributions.
  - `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` adapted from Contributor Covenant with Nexus-specific emphasis on humanistic values, privacy, decentralization, and technical depth.
  - `docs/ecosystem.md` — full mapping of related repositories (nexus-daemon, novanet, nexus-monitoring, nexus-portal), their roles, interconnections, and contribution paths.
  - `docs/architecture.md` — expanded layered architecture with principles, interactions/nuances, threat considerations, scalability trade-offs, and evolution guidance.
  - `docs/roadmap.md` — phased milestones (Phase 0–4) with success metrics, concrete examples, edge cases, and cross-cutting concerns.
  - `docs/security.md` — detailed threat model (STRIDE per layer), mitigations, privacy engineering, cryptography, compliance (GDPR, radio equipment in EU), incident response, and open questions.
- MIT License and foundational `.gitignore`.
- Cross-linking and positioning of Nexus as the central integration hub for mesh networking (xMesh/NovaNet/QNET), blockchain (XCoin/QCoin), AI swarms, hardware prototypes (Soilnova, Vista Nova, etc.), and self-improving systems.

### Changed
- Repository description and topics refined to emphasize foundational hub role and integration across decentralized technologies.

### Notes
- This phase establishes the documentation and governance baseline so parallel development in sibling repositories can proceed coherently.
- All documentation follows a thorough style: context, examples, nuances, implications, edge cases, and related considerations.
